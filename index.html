The Origin & Brain Trust

The Story Behind Kenweb Services

Kenweb Services was founded by Ikenna, a dedicated Statistics student. Recognizing a massive gap in data collection accuracy and digital architecture among growing organizations, Ikenna leveraged his rigorous academic background in statistical analysis to build robust data collection pipelines.

Teaming up with Michael, our visionary CMO who co-brainstormed our core technological innovations, and Amanda, our brilliant Content Creator who shapes our brand narrative, Kenweb Services has evolved into a premier agency delivering top-tier software development, database integration, web maintenance, and AI solutions.
